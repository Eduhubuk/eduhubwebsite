Documentation checklist

  • Original valid passport — high-quality scanned colour copies
  • Academic transcripts — 10th & 12th grade marksheets and passing certificates
  • Financial proof — bank statements covering the last 3 months
  • Medical fitness certificate from a registered doctor
  • Police clearance certificate (PCC)
  • 4–6 recent passport-size colour photographs

Programmes offered

Programme structure & curriculum.

A comprehensive MBA pathway designed to build strong business fundamentals and strategic leadership skills.

Module 1: Strategic Planning

Develop, evaluate and review organisational strategies, considering internal and external factors.

Module 2: Finance for Strategic Managers

Analyse financial data to inform strategic decision-making, including financial statements, ratio analysis and capital expenditure proposals.

Module 3: Research for Strategic Development

Produce research proposals, apply research methodologies, present findings and evaluate research skills.

Module 4: Organisational Behaviour

How leadership, structure, culture, motivation and creativity influence an organisation's effectiveness and efficiency.

Module 5: Personal Development for Leadership and Strategic Management

Develop leadership and management skills to create and achieve organisational vision and strategy.

Module 6: International Business Environment

How globalisation, international trade and diverse international markets impact business operations.

Module 7: Strategic Marketing

Developing strategic marketing plans, understanding consumer behaviour and aligning marketing with business objectives.

Module 8: Strategic Human Resource Management

HR strategies and their impact on organisational efficiency, aligned with business objectives.

Module 1: Management Project Methods

Research methods, project proposal development and ethical considerations.

Module 2: Strategic Management Project

Strategic decision-making, project implementation and leadership.
